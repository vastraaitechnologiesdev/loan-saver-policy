Privacy Policy

Loan Prepayment & Tenor Saver • Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Loan Prepayment & Tenor Saver ("the App") is developed and maintained by V Astra AI Technologies as a 100% offline, privacy-first financial utility tool. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identity details, banking information, device identifiers, or contact lists.
  • 100% On-Device Computation: All loan details, interest rates, tenure calculations, and prepayment entries entered into the App are computed entirely on your device.
  • Local Calculation History: Any saved calculation history remains strictly on your device's internal private storage (local preferences). It is never synced to external servers or accessible to third parties.

3. Zero Permissions Required

The App does not require or request sensitive runtime permissions such as Internet access, Camera, Storage/Media, Location, or Contacts. It operates completely offline without external network communication.

4. Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs), analytics trackers, cloud databases, or third-party advertising networks.

5. Data Deletion

Since all calculation history is stored purely in local device memory, clearing the App's data from your device settings or uninstalling the App immediately and permanently erases all saved entries.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted with an updated "Last updated" date.
